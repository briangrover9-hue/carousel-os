# Readers and venues

Where a deck is read changes what a slide has to do. Add what you learn about real readers here, one line each, with the date.

| Venue | What it asks of a slide |
|---|---|
| Emailed file, read alone | The headline carries the point. Notes and source lines do the explaining. |
| Screen share in a meeting | Short text, large type, one idea. A presenter fills in the rest. |
| Printed handout | Source lines and bases must be readable at page size. Color cannot carry meaning alone. |
| Stage or room | Very little text. The headline is the slide. |
