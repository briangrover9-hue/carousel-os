# Wiki index

- [lessons.md](lessons.md): rules and live lessons, edited only by `node scripts/lessons.js`.
- [glossary.md](glossary.md): the few terms the commands use.
- [readers-and-venues.md](readers-and-venues.md): what we know about who reads our decks and where, and what that does to a slide.
- [sources-and-windows.md](sources-and-windows.md): what we know about the evidence behind our decks and how windows get labeled.
- [deck-decisions.md](deck-decisions.md): standing decisions about how we build decks, with the reason for each.
